# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in **QCA Spacetime Lab** a harassment-free, welcoming, and scientifically constructive experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, rigorous, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy research and software community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing scientific viewpoints, numerical methods, and constructive critiques
- Giving and gracefully accepting technical feedback grounded in reproducible evidence
- Taking responsibility for mistakes, correcting errors transparently, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for scientific rigor and the broader community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional or academic setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, and correspondence related to this repository), and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainer at **johan.m.cabrera.ch@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
